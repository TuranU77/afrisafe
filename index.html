FAQ

Your questions.

Who is AFRISAV for?

We support German businesses with operations, projects or employee assignments in Africa, particularly small and medium-sized companies.

What does the network bring together?

WOLDEAB Technology Consulting contributes safety engineering and HSE expertise. Hafenpraxis Dr. Schlaich & Dr. Beyer contributes occupational and travel medicine.

Can support focus on a specific need?

We discuss the services you need and agree a suitable scope, from individual training or audits to combined HSE and medical support.

Which locations can you support?

Tell us where your business operates. We assess the requirements and possible support for the specific country, location and project.

How does cooperation start?

An initial conversation helps us understand your situation. We then agree priorities, services and the next steps with you.
